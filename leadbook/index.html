How to read this

Pavement on site is every paved square yard inside the parcel line, buildings removed. On lots where a whole-lot job was done in 2026, this measure came within about 10% of the invoiced square yards.

Section to price is the share of the lot showing distress, because owners buy one section at a time. Likely ticket applies your own 2026 unit pricing to that section: $22 and $44 per square yard are the middle half of your jobs, $29 the median. A range to open a conversation, not a quote.

Condition is judged from the photo by independent reviewers working from a written rubric and a reference sheet of real examples. Every warm lot and the top 60 Methuen lots were rated twice. Reviewers agreed on the exact band about two thirds of the time and were almost never more than one band apart. The ordering is more reliable than the label.

Photography is spring 2025, leaves off. Anything repaved since will look worse here than it is on the ground.
