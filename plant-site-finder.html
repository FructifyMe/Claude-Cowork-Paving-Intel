Two tracks. Permitted & operating sites are working quarries and pits held by independent operators — the play is a purchase, partnership, or co-location on land that already has the use established. Idle / motivated seller sites are pits that have gone intermittent, idled, or abandoned in federal records, or are on the market — the play is a cheaper buy where the permitting history still helps.

Where the data comes from. MSHA's national mine registry (every sand, gravel and stone operation with status and operator), the MassGIS statewide parcel layer (owner of record, acreage, assessed value, last sale, zoning; use codes 410–413), and public web sources for each of the twenty (company sites, town filings, news, state corporate registries). New Hampshire's parcel layer carries no owner names, so NH sites show the MSHA operator instead of a deed owner. Portable crushers and municipal pits were removed.

Next pass. Zoning and aquifer-protection screens, MassDOT/NHDOT approved-plant map (competitors and acquisition targets), owner phone enrichment on the sites the owner wants to pursue, and his own weights on the rubric.
